How VA math works

VA does not simply add disability percentages. Under 38 CFR § 4.25, multiple ratings are combined using the remaining-efficiency method and rounded to the nearest 10% for the final evaluation. The bilateral factor can apply when qualifying compensable disabilities affect both arms, both legs, or paired skeletal muscles. The factor is calculated before the bilateral group is combined with other ratings.

The dollar estimate uses the 2026 VA compensation tables and includes qualifying dependent additions. Check VA's official compensation tables for your exact dependent category, and use our back-pay calculator for retroactive estimates.
