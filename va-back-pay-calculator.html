How the estimate works

Schoolchildren are calculated using the VA schoolchild add-on. The tool assumes qualifying dependents and Aid and Attendance remained eligible for every selected month. Changes during the period require separate calculations. The estimate assumes VA already included eligible dependents in the payments it made.

VA generally cannot begin payment before the first day of the month following the effective date under 38 CFR § 3.31. The calculator sums the monthly difference between the correct and previously paid compensation using the VA rate table in effect for each benefit month, including the December cost-of-living adjustment. It assumes VA already paid the applicable dependent additions at the previous rating. If VA did not pay those additions, actual back pay may differ.

It uses published 2023–2026 rates and estimated historical rates for 2000–2022. Historical amounts are extrapolated using annual COLA percentages and have not been verified against each VA historical table. For earlier years, treat the result as a rough estimate only and supports eligible spouses, dependent parents, minor children, schoolchildren, and spouse Aid and Attendance. It does not determine legal eligibility, effective dates, staged ratings, dependent changes, SMC, withholding, military retired pay offsets, recoupment, or representative fees. It is an estimate, not a VA award determination.
